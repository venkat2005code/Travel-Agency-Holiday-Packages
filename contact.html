How far in advance should I book my trip?
For most luxury itineraries, we recommend initiating the planning process 6-9 months in advance. For peak seasons in highly sought-after destinations (such as Kyoto during cherry blossom season or African safaris during the great migration), 12 months is highly recommended to secure exclusive properties.
Do you handle commercial flights?
Yes. Our dedicated air desk manages all commercial first and business class ticketing, as well as private aviation charters. We handle all routing, seat selection, and monitor for delays to ensure seamless connections.
Are the itineraries flexible once booked?
Absolutely. The hallmark of a Trek journey is flexibility. While some core components (like specific lodge reservations) are fixed, our on-the-ground concierges can adjust daily activities, dining reservations, and tour times on the fly to suit your mood.
