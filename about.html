The Legacy

Our Journey

From a singular vision to a global standard in luxury travel.

2018

The Inception

Trek is founded in a small London office by two former National Geographic guides with a vision to make remote travel accessible and luxurious.

2021

Global Expansion

We secure Series A funding and open dedicated concierge hubs in New York, Dubai, and Singapore, partnering with over 500 elite luxury stays.

2026

The Digital Platform

We launch the Trek Customer Dashboard, completely revolutionizing how bespoke itineraries are built, shared, and experienced live on the road.

Our Mission

To completely remove friction from global exploration, empowering our clients to immerse themselves in new cultures safely, luxuriously, and authentically.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted curator of extraordinary human experiences, setting the standard for sustainable, tech-enabled luxury travel.
